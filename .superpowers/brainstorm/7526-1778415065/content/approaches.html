C — Compact accordion + ⌘K palette

Same single-column form but: only one section open at a time, denser controls, ⌘K command palette to jump straight to any control by name. Tier groups merge into a single Emphasis section. Smallest visual change, biggest "I know what I want" win.

My recommendation: A (tabbed right rail). It directly fixes 1 (less visible at once), 3 (preview gets the stage), and 4 (Emphasis tab dedups tiers); 2 (find a specific control) is solved by the tab switching being only 4 buttons away rather than 30+ scroll-pixels. B is more elegant but the per-frame Player overlay makes click-to-edit-tier hard to do well. C helps but doesn't really fix the cramped-preview complaint.
